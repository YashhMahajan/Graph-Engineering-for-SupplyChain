# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

All members, contributors, and leaders of this project must keep participation free of
harassment for all persons. This applies to all persons, independent of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Correct behavior:

- Show empathy and kindness to other persons.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes. Apologize to the persons that they affect.
- Do what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention of all types.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publication of private information of other persons without their permission.
- Other behavior that is not correct in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer, through a private GitHub
security advisory or a direct message to @YashhMahajan. The maintainer examines all
reports quickly and fairly.
